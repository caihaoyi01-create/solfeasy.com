## Seven-day money-back guarantee

You can request a refund within seven days of your first paid piano lesson subscription purchase. Open a billing request in [account support](/settings/tickets) and include the order number from your billing history. Do not send card numbers or passwords.

## Free trial

The seven-day trial does not require a payment card and does not automatically turn into a paid subscription. You will only be charged after choosing a paid plan and completing the payment provider's checkout.

## Cancellation

Manage or cancel an active subscription in [billing settings](/settings/billing). Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period. Cancellation and a refund request are separate actions.

## Processing a request

Support checks the order and purchase date, then processes eligible refunds to the original payment method. Your payment provider determines when the returned funds appear. Requests after the guarantee window and renewal-related issues are reviewed through account support. This policy does not limit any rights that apply to your purchase.
