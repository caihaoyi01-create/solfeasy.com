## Free practice and essential storage

You can use the music note tools without creating an account. An essential browser cookie identifies your anonymous practice session so the service can apply the daily allowance and remember results. We store answered questions, daily totals, streaks and a result-visibility preference. Audio is synthesized in your browser; the tools do not access your microphone or record your playing.

## Public practice results

Practice highlights show anonymous score totals and best streaks. They do not include your name, email or account identifier. Chord discoveries show only musical choices such as root, chord type and inversion. These learning results are public by default. Pro users can set their results to private using the visibility control beside the results. A previously selected private preference is preserved; a new private chord discovery is not silently published when a trial expires.

## Account and course information

If you register, we store the account details needed to sign you in, including your name, email and a protected password credential. We also store trial dates and completed lessons so you can continue learning on another device. A temporary exercise draft may be saved in the current browser tab while you sign in, then removed after it is saved to your account.

## Billing and support

Payment information is handled by the payment provider used at checkout. The service retains order and subscription records needed to grant access and resolve billing requests; it does not store full payment-card numbers. Support requests contain the information you choose to send. Do not include passwords or full card details in a ticket.

## Optional services

Advertising or analytics may be enabled by the site operator. When active, these providers may process device and usage information under their own policies and the consent choices offered on the deployed site. The local preview has no advertising or analytics provider configured. Essential account and practice functionality does not require advertising.

## Managing your information

Use account settings to update your profile and account support to request access, correction or removal of account-related information. Anonymous practice data is associated with the browser cookie rather than a public identity. Clearing that cookie removes the browser's connection to those records; it does not itself delete server records. Data is retained while needed to provide the service, support users and maintain billing records.

## Contact and changes

For a privacy request, use [account support](/settings/tickets). We update the date above when this policy changes and communicate material changes through the service where appropriate.
